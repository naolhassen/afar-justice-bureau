The Regional Justice Sector Offices teamed up with the Regional Education Bureau to give learning materials to 200 students at Boina School in Dubti.

የክልሉ ፍትህ ሴክተር መሥሪያ ቤቶች ከክልሉ ትምህርት ቢሮ ጋር በመተባበር በዱብቲ ወረዳ ቦይና ትምህርት ቤት ለ200 ተማሪዎች የመማሪያ ቁሳቁስ ድጋፍ አደረጉ።

Rakaakayak Qadli sekterih k/buxaaxi Rakaakayak barittô biirolluk cattiimak Dubti daqaarak Boyna barittô buxal 200 yakke barteynitih barittô mansoofa aben.

Heads of Justice Bureaus and other folks from all over came to Samara to join the 10th Ministry of Justice panel discussion on 'A New Criminal Law Procedure for a Stronger Justice System,' happening tomorrow in Samara.

“አዲስ የወንጀል ሕግ ሥነ-ሥርዓት ለጠንካራ ፍትሕ ሥርዓት” በሚል መሪ ሃሳብ ነገ በሰመራ ከተማ የሚካሄደውን 10ኛው የፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር ፓናል ውይይት ለመታደም ከሁሉም ክልሎች የተወጣጡ የፍትሕ ቢሮ ኃላፊዎችና ሌሎች ባለድርሻ አካላት ሰመራ ገብተዋል።

Qadli biiroh saqolti kee kalah tan wagsiisah yan dagorti kulli rakaakayak tewqeh tan qadli malaakak 10 heele paanel walal beera samara magaalal "qusba baatileyni madqah gexsit maqar le qadli siiratah" inta mattaco ugutak qadli malaakak 10 heele paanel walal yassakaxxeenim samara fan temeete.

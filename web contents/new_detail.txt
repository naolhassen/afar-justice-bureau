ዳይሬክቶሬቶች
 
1.  የዐቃብያነ ህግ አስተዳደር ጉባኤ ጽ/ቤት
የጉባኤ ጽ/ቤቱን እቅድና በጀት በማዘጋጀት ለፍትህ ቢሮ ማቅረብ፤ ተከታትሎ ማጸደቅ፣ ሲፈቀድም ተግባራዊ ማድረግ፤ የስራ አፈጻጸም ሪፖርትም ወቅቱን ጠብቆ መገምገም፤ ለሚመለከተው የስራ ክፍል ማቅረብ፡፡
2.  የምህረትና ይቅርታ ቦርድ ጽ/ቤት ተግባርና ኃላፊነት
የጽ/ቤቱን ዕቅድ ማዘጋጀት፤ የስራ አፈጻጸም ሪፖርትም ወቅቱን ጠብቆ መገምገም፤ ለሚመለከተው የስራ ክፍል ማቅረብ፤ በቀረቡ አቤቱታዎች ላይ ምላሽ መስጠት፤ ምላሽ እንዲሰጥ ማድረግ፤ አፈጻጸማቸውን መከታተል፣ በጽ/ቤቱ ስር ላሉ ባለሙያዎችና ሰራተኞች የዓመት ፈቃድ መስጠት፡፡
3.  የፍልሰት ትብብር ጥምረት ጽ/ቤት
ከንዑስ የስራ ቡድኖች አባል ተቋማት ጋር በመተባበር ክልላዊ የፀረ-ሕገ-ወጥ የሰዎች ዝውውር ወንጀል መከላከል ግብረ-ሃይልን እቅድ የማዘጋጀትና ዕቅዱን ለአያንዳንዱ ፈጻሚ ተቋማት እንዲደርስ ማድረግ፡፡
4.  የወንጀል ዳይሬክቶሬት
ተለይቶ በተሰጣቸው የወንጀል ጉዳዮች መሰረት በስራቸው ያሉ የወንጀል ጉዳይ አደረጃጀቶችን፣ የዞን ፍትህ መምሪያዎችን፣ የወረዳ ፍትህ ጽ/ቤቶችን መደገፍ፣ ማስተባበር፣ መምራት፤ ዕቅድ ማዘጋጀት፤ የስራ አፈጻጸም ሪፖርት ወቅቱን ጠብቆ መገምገም፤ ለሚመለከተው የስራ ክፍል ማቅረብ፡፡
5.  የሙስናና ኢኮኖሚ ነክ ወንጀል ጉዳዮች ዳይሬክቶሬት
የሙስናና ኢኮኖሚ ነክ ወንጀል ጉዳዮች አደረጃጀቶችን ማለትም የዞን ፍትህ መምሪያዎችን መደገፍ፣ ማስተባበር፣ መምራት፤ ዕቅድ ማዘጋጀት፤ የስራ አፈጻጸም ሪፖርት ወቅቱን ጠብቆ መገምገም፤ ለሚመለከተው የስራ ክፍል ማቅረብ፡፡
 
 
6.  የልዩ ልዩ ወንጀል ጉዳዮች ዳይሬክቶሬት
በዳይሬክቶሬቱ ስልጣን ስር የተሰጡ ወንጀሎች ስለመፈጸማቸው በቂ ጥርጣሬ ሲኖሩ  ወይም ጥቆማ በቀረበ ጊዜ ተገቢው ምርመራ ተጣርቶና በበቂ ማስረጃ ተደግፎ እንዲቀርብ ማድረግ፤ ክስ በቀረበባቸው ጉዳዮች ላይ ምስክሮች የሚያስረዱትን ተገንዝቦ፣ የክርክር ፍሬ ነገሮችን ከቀጠሮው በፊት አጥንቶና ተዘጋጅቶ በመቅረብ መከራከር፡፡
7.  የፍትሐብሔር ጉዳዮች ዳይሬክቶሬት
የፍትሐብሔር ጉዳዮች አደረጃጀቶችን ማለትም የዞን ፍትህ መምሪያዎችን እና የወረዳ ፍትህ ጽ/ቤቶችን መደገፍ፣ ማስተባበር፣ መምራት፤ ዕቅድ ማዘጋጀት፤ የስራ አፈጻጸም ሪፖርት ወቅቱን ጠብቆ መገምገም፤ ለሚመለከተው የስራ ክፍል ማቅረብ፡፡
8.  የህግ ጥናት፣ማርቀቅና ማስረጽ ዳይሬክቶሬት
ዕቅድ ማዘጋጀት፣ የስራ አፈጻጸም ሪፖርት ወቅቱን ጠብቆ መገምገምና ለሚመለከተው የስራ ክፍል ማቅረብ፤ በዳይሬክቶሬቱ ውስጥ ባለው የሰው ኃይል እውቀት፣ ክህሎትና አመለካከት ላይ የሚታዩ ክፍተቶችን በመቅረፍ ለውጤት ማብቃት፡፡
9.  የሰብዓዊ መብት ጉዳዮችና የማህበረሰብ ግንዛቤ ማስጨበጫ ዳይሬክቶሬት
የሰብዓዊ መብት ጉዳዮችና የማህበረሰብ ግንዛቤ ማስጨበጫ ዳይሬክቶሬት አደረጃጀቶችን መደገፍ፣ ማስተባበር፣ መምራት፤ ዕቅድ ማዘጋጀት፤ የስራ አፈጻጸም ሪፖርት ወቅቱን ጠብቆ መገምገም፤ ለሚመለከተው የስራ ክፍል ማቅረብ፡፡

Directorates
1. Office of the Prosecutors' Administration Conference 
Get the conference office's plan and budget ready and send it over to the Justice Office, follow up to get it approved, put it into action once approved, keep an eye on the performance report, and forward it to the relevant department. 
 
2. Duties and Responsibilities of the Office of the Board of Mercy and Pardons 
Put together a plan for the Office, check the performance report regularly, send it to the right department, handle complaints, make sure responses are given, monitor how things are going, and manage annual leave for the professionals and staff under the Office. 
 
3. Office of Migration Cooperation Coalition 
Come up with a plan for the Regional Anti-Trafficking Crime Prevention Task Force in collaboration with the members of the Sub-Working Groups and share the plan with each agency that will implement it.
4. Criminal Directorate
Helping out, coordinating, directing, making plans, keeping track of performance reports, and sending them to the right department for the specified criminal cases.
 
5. Directorate of Corruption and Economic Crimes
Helping out, coordinating, directing, making plans, checking performance reports, and sending them to the proper justice departments.
 
6. Directorate of Miscellaneous Criminal Cases
Making sure that when there’s enough suspicion about crimes under this Directorate’s authority, or when someone suggests so, the right investigation is carried out and backed up with enough evidence.
7. Directorate of Civil Affairs
They support, coordinate, guide, plan, review performance reports, and send them to the relevant departments.
 
8. Directorate of Legal Studies, Drafting and Drafting
They make plans, review performance reports on time, and submit them to the relevant department; they also work on improving the knowledge, skills, and attitudes of their staff and make sure results are achieved.
 
9. Directorate of Human Rights Affairs and Community Awareness
They support, coordinate, guide, plan, review performance reports, and send them to the relevant department.
 
10. Directorate of Documents, Bar Associations and Charities Affairs

They support, coordinate, guide, plan, carry out tasks, review performance reports on time, and submit them to the relevant department.

[9/28/2026 6:42 PM] Abdo Hantuta: Powers and Responsibilities

Regarding studying and training in law
They do research on how well things work, set up and manage a system for collecting criminal justice data, and provide training and education to help prosecutors improve their mindset, knowledge, and skills. 

Regarding drafting and organizing laws
They handle drafting laws issued by the regional government, prepare draft laws when asked by regional government offices, make sure drafts from government bodies follow the Constitution and both federal and state laws, review legal changes, and compile and summarize laws.

Regarding the Criminal Case  
This involves putting criminal justice policies into action; starting, overseeing, following up, and coordinating federal criminal investigations if the state has jurisdiction or has delegated the authority; submitting follow-up reports and making sure the investigations are properly wrapped up; deciding whether to stop or continue investigations; making sure everything is done legally. It also includes prosecuting criminal cases under state jurisdiction, handling federal criminal cases delegated by the Ministry of Justice to the Regional Bureau of Justice, deciding not to prosecute, closing cases, arguing cases, and prosecuting those involved. Additionally, it’s responsible for recovering related assets, carrying out court decisions and orders, and keeping an eye on compliance. On behalf of the regional government, it handles national debates on civil matters to protect the rights and interests of the people and regional authorities, monitors compliance, prepares and negotiates contracts for major regional projects and other matters of public interest, investigates and makes decisions on civil issues when regional offices aren’t in contact, represents citizens in legal matters if they have the financial means, and coordinates the recovery of assets related to crime.
Regarding the Enforcement of Federal and State Laws
It handles making sure federal and regional laws are applied consistently, that regional government offices follow the law, and provides legal training to officials, candidates, employees, and private sector participants when needed.

Regarding Human Rights
It’s in charge of creating human rights education and awareness programs, providing independent legal aid, implementing a national human rights action plan, visiting police stations and prisons to ensure legal treatment, addressing illegal practices, enforcing international human rights treaties, handling inquiries, and reporting to relevant authorities.

Regarding the Registration and Follow-up of Lawyers and Associations
It’s authorized to verify documents, handle registration, licensing, supervision, and management of advocacy services, and oversee associations.

Regarding the Coordination of Accountable Institutions
It coordinates building and debugging works and manages the registration of events.
